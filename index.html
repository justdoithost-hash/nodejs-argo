Types of Fishing

🌊

Freshwater Fishing

Enjoy lakes, rivers, and ponds targeting bass, trout, and catfish. Perfect for beginners and family outings.

⚓

Saltwater Fishing

From shore casting to deep-sea charters, chase marlin, tuna, and snapper in the open ocean.

🪰

Fly Fishing

An elegant technique using artificial flies to catch trout and salmon in clear, flowing streams.

🧊

Ice Fishing

Drill a hole in frozen lakes and drop a line for perch and walleye during winter months.

Essential Gear

🎣

Rod & Reel

Choose a spinning combo for versatility, or a baitcaster for precision casting.

🪝

Hooks & Lures

Carry a variety of sizes — soft plastics, crankbaits, spinners, and live bait.

🧵

Line

Monofilament for stretch and forgiveness; braided line for strength and sensitivity.

🎒

Tackle Box

Keep pliers, sinkers, bobbers, and extra hooks organized and ready to go.

Top Tips for Beginners

  • Check the weather & water: Fish are more active during low-light hours — dawn and dusk are prime times.
  • Match the hatch: Use lures and bait that resemble the local forage fish or insects.
  • Be patient and quiet: Noise and sudden movements can spook fish quickly.
  • Learn your local regulations: Always check licensing rules, size limits, and catch limits.
  • Practice catch and release: Wet your hands, handle gently, and return fish quickly to preserve populations.
